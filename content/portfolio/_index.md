# Portfolio

> **From foggy problems to working structures.**

The domains change. The way I enter them is much more consistent.

**Understand the context → map the structure → design what is needed → make it usable in the real world.**

---

## 01 · 🌦️ AI Forecast Support
### Turning an unfamiliar domain into technical direction

I traced forecasters' workflows and existing tools, then helped turn underdefined needs into clearer requirements and technical direction.

- Domain Understanding
- Requirement Definition
- Technical Planning
- Cross-functional Communication

[Read Case Study →](/portfolio/ai-forecast-support/)

---

## 02 · 🧪 Chemi.lol
### Turning a relationship between two players into features

I designed relationship-level features from differences in KDA, KP, GPM, Vision, Win Rate, and lane combinations,  
then compared Random Forest, Ridge, SVR, XGBoost, and MLP/ensemble approaches.

[Read Case Study →](/portfolio/chemi-lol/)

### Turning a research question into a repeatable data pipeline

A research-engineering project spanning Riot API collection, consecutive-play sampling, Stroop Test tooling, dynamic-data exploration, and participant reporting.

[Read Case Study →](/portfolio/lol-player-research/)
