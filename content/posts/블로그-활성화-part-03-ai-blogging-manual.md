#### info4blog 작성

`info4blog 갱신 (마지막 작성일: YYYY-MM-DD)`

#### Updating `info4blog`

Send Claude the following trigger:

`Update info4blog (last updated: YYYY-MM-DD)`

`Update info4blog`
